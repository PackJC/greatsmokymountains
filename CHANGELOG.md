# Changelog

## 2.0.0

The map rebuilt from real data at real scale.

### Added
- Real terrain: 20 x 20 km on a 5 m height grid, from 327 m in the valleys to 2,010 m on Mount Le Conte.
- Roads, hiking trails, creeks, buildings and place names from OpenStreetMap: 560 km of roads and 264 km of trails, including 23 km of the Appalachian Trail, plus 45 km of rivers and about 720 km of streams and brooks fitted to the valley floors.
- Gatlinburg: about 1,800 of its mapped buildings as vanilla models, more than 1,000 rental cabins, fuel stations, street lamps and mailboxes, the Gatlinburg SkyBridge, the Ober Mountain Aerial Tramway, the Gatlinburg Sky Lift and two chair lifts.
- Trail blazes, footbridges over the creeks, and National Park Service style signs at trailheads and junctions.
- More than 3 million trees in zones by elevation and slope, with laurel thickets, dead and fallen firs, and a wildflower meadow on the open ground.
- Cliff faces, outcrops and boulders, waterfalls, ponds, springs and water pumps.
- Furniture at overlooks, trailheads, picnic areas, campsites, shelters and ranger stations, and orchards at the old homesteads.
- A mission with the loot economy, player spawns and events, a paper map and an intro scene.
- Hungarian, and the paper map's description in every language.

### Changed
- New mod logo in the same style as Geb's other mods: the real skyline from Gatlinburg to Mount Le Conte, traced from the map's own heights, white in a black frame and smoky blue when hovered.

### Fixed
- The server and client logs no longer show "ANIMATION (E): Can't load …/Anims/cfg/skeletons.anim.xml" on every start.
