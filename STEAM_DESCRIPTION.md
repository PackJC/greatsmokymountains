[h1]Mod Information[/h1]
Great Smoky Mountains is a [b]20 x 20 km map of the Smokies at real scale[/b]: Gatlinburg, Pittman Center, Mount Le Conte and Newfound Gap. The ground is the real terrain, and the roads, hiking trails, creeks, buildings and place names come from OpenStreetMap, so the map is laid out like the real place.

[b]Source, mission files and setup:[/b] [url=https://github.com/PackJC/greatsmokymountains]github.com/PackJC/greatsmokymountains[/url]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[list]
[*] DayZ 1.30.
[*] No other mods. Load it on both the server and every client.
[/list]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]Real terrain at real scale:[/b] 20 x 20 km on a 5 m height grid, from 327 m in the valleys to 2,010 m on Mount Le Conte.
[*] [b]Towns and places:[/b] Gatlinburg, Pittman Center, Cartertown, Glade, Pinnacle, Sugarlands, Twin Creeks and Zion Grove, with ranger stations, LeConte Lodge, campgrounds, RV resorts, backcountry campsites and trail shelters.
[*] [b]Gatlinburg as it is:[/b] about 1,800 of its mapped buildings as vanilla models, more than 1,000 rental cabins, fuel stations, street lamps and mailboxes, the Gatlinburg SkyBridge, and the Ober Mountain Aerial Tramway, the Gatlinburg Sky Lift and two chair lifts.
[*] [b]Roads and hiking trails:[/b] 560 km of roads and 264 km of trails, including 23 km of the Appalachian Trail, Alum Cave, Boulevard, Rainbow Falls and Trillium Gap. The trails have painted blazes, footbridges over the creeks, and National Park Service style signs at trailheads and junctions.
[*] [b]Water:[/b] 45 km of rivers and about 720 km of streams and brooks laid along the valley floors, with waterfalls, ponds, creek boulders, springs and water pumps.
[*] [b]The forest:[/b] more than 3 million trees in zones by elevation and slope, from cove hardwoods and oak-pine to the spruce-fir on the high peaks, with laurel thickets, dead and fallen firs, and a wildflower meadow on the open ground.
[*] [b]Cliffs and rock[/b] placed from the terrain's slopes and the mapped cliffs and peaks.
[*] [b]Overlooks, picnic areas and farms:[/b] furniture at the overlooks, trailheads and picnic areas, and orchards at the old homesteads.
[*] [b]Ready to run:[/b] a mission with the loot economy, player spawns and events, a paper map, and an intro scene.
[/list]

[h1]Server Setup[/h1]
[list]
[*] Copy mission/dayzOffline.greatsmokymountains from the GitHub page into your server's mpmissions folder.
[*] In serverDZ.cfg, set the mission template to dayzOffline.greatsmokymountains.
[*] Add -mod=@greatsmokymountains to the launch parameters and copy the .bikey into your keys folder.
[/list]
